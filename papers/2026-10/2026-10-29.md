### 1. **[LinMix: Data Mixing as Linear Domain Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37390v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Hubert Baniecki, Przemyslaw Biecek, Martin Pawelczyk
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.CL` `stat.ML`
- **AI摘要**: 提出LinMix数据混合方法以优化预训练
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Language model performance depends strongly on the mixture of domains in the pretraining data corpus. Popular data mixing methods select these domain weights by training many small proxy models on random mixes and then fitting a regression model to predict performance from the mix. We recognize this practice as a domain attribution problem, where the regression measures each domain's contribution to the target metric. Through this lens, the standard Dirichlet sampling of proxy data mixes is a poor regression design that requires tuning and cannot support a linear fit for hundreds of domains. Building on classical optimal experimental design, we introduce LinMix, which samples mixes at the vertices of the capped simplex so that each domain is at its cap or absent. This hyperparameter-free vertex sampling decorrelates domain effects, enabling an interpretable linear model whose coefficients are the domain attributions. The optimal mix given by LinMix provably keeps only domains with positive attribution values and requires no search over candidate mixes. We further establish the optimality of vertex sampling, and prove that Dirichlet sampling inflates the attribution error when its mixes concentrate around the prior data mix. Pretraining experiments with 1B-parameter OLMo2 and 4B Qwen3.5 language models show that LinMix outperforms Dirichlet-based data mixing across reasoning, code, and math benchmarks, with relative gains of 46% on MBPP and 14% on GSM8K. At 288 fine-grained domains, only vertex sampling supports a linear fit, and LinMix improves benchmark accuracy by 11% on average over the best nonlinear baseline.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 创新性结合最优实验设计，理论和实验扎实

### 2. **[Request-Adaptive Execution for Efficient Hybrid LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37432v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 79/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Fahao Chen, Xuzhe Yan, Qi Zhang et al.
- **标签**: `cs.DC`
- **AI摘要**: 提出支持请求自适应混合大模型推理的Morph系统
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Hybrid large language models (LLMs) have emerged as a promising way to combine the modeling capability of the Attention mechanism with the efficiency of state space models (SSMs). However, most existing hybrid LLMs still inherit a static design philosophy: once the Attention-to-SSM layout is chosen, every request must follow the same hybrid execution. This one-layout-fits-all design is inefficient for real-world serving workloads, where different requests rely on contextual information in different ways. This paper presents Morph, a serving system that co-designs the model architecture and runtime for request-adaptive hybrid LLM inference. Its model architecture pairs Attention and SSM layers within each block and uses prefill-oriented gating to select a request-specific layout that remains fixed throughout decoding. Its serving runtime preserves batching efficiency under divergent layer choices through selective batching and enables correct prefix reuse through hybrid-aware context caching. Experiments on six datasets show that Morph achieves a better quality-efficiency trade-off than existing static hybrid baselines. System-level evaluation further shows that Morph improves the serving throughput by up to 3.87x over baselines while substantially reducing inference latency.
</details>

### 3. **[Heads of Security: Learned Attention Gates Improve Prompt Injection Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37657v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 75/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Igor Shilov, Xiaoxue Yang, Alexander Panfilov et al.
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.CL` `cs.CR`
- **AI摘要**: 引入注意力门控机制提升提示词注入防御能力
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

We introduce Heads of Security (HoS), a prompt injection defense that enforces instruction-data separation via a small set of learned per-head attention gates. HoS learns how each head should redistribute attention between trusted and untrusted tokens, enabling models to reason over untrusted content without following injected instructions. We evaluate HoS on Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct and GPT-OSS-20B. We compare it against SecAlign++, a state-of-the-art finetuning-based defense, and ASIDE, a defense that, like ours, builds instruction-data separation into the model architecture. We show that HoS offers greater robustness to adaptive attacks, better performance on standard utility benchmarks, and matches top performance on standard agentic prompt injection benchmarks. More specifically, the black-box RL-Hammer attack reaches 97% ASR against GPT-OSS-20B and 85% against SecAlign, but only 10% against HoS. Claudini, a state-of-the-art white-box attack, strengthened against HoS through autoresearch, requires 3 times more FLOPs to reach the same ASR on HoS as it does on GPT-OSS-20B or SecAlign. HoS preserves base-model utility across standard multiple-choice benchmarks and prompting setups, whereas SecAlign and ASIDE degrade in few-shot settings. On agentic benchmarks with static attacks, HoS and SecAlign perform similarly and substantially improve the security-utility trade-off over the base model. ASIDE struggles with tool use, achieving only 10% utility. These results show that prompt injection robustness can be achieved and improved through targeted attention interventions.
</details>

### 4. **[MuonGS: Pretraining LLMs with Relaxed Weight-Norm Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37647v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 75/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Athanasios Glentis, Dawei Li, Mingyi Hong
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.AI` `math.OC`
- **AI摘要**: 提出MuonGS优化器用于大模型预训练
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Weight decay and explicit weight-norm constraints improve optimizer performance in language model pretraining, yet a unified understanding of why such control helps and how much is needed remains incomplete. We analyze norm control in Muon over the entire training trajectory using the \emph{accumulated effective learning rate}, $C_T=\sum_{t&lt;T}η_t/\|W_t\|_F$, where $η_t$ is the nominal learning rate and $η_t/\|W_t\|_F$ is the effective learning rate relative to the weight norm. For bounded update directions, accumulation over any training interval bounds relative weight displacement. We show that vanilla Muon can accumulate only logarithmically, potentially limiting exploration and helping explain its slower empirical progress, whereas MuonW and MuonH guarantee at least linear accumulation under the stated conditions. Going beyond bounded-norm control, we show that sufficiently slow norm growth guarantees faster accumulation than the logarithmic worst case, opening up less restrictive optimizer designs. Based on this insight, we propose MuonGS, which uses Gram--Schmidt (GS) projection to remove the radial component of each Muon update without fixing the weight norm. With bounded update directions and a constant nominal learning rate, we prove that the weight norm grows at most proportionally to $\sqrt{t}$ after $t$ steps. Consequently, MuonGS guarantees $C_T=Ω(\sqrt{T})$, with accumulation sustained even in later training. Further, for smooth scale-invariant objectives we establish its $O(T^{-1/2})$ stationarity guarantee. In pretraining experiments with 160M- and 400M-parameter dense models as well as a 135M active- and 1B total- parameter MoE model, MuonGS achieves lower validation perplexity than MuonH at long training horizons. In particular, for the dense model it achieves an estimated token-equivalent speedup up to about $15\%$ at matched validation loss to MuonH.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 理论分析严谨且有性能提升，但属于优化器改进

### 5. **[Multi-State Linear Attention with Generalized Second-Order Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37645v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 75/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-27
- **作者**: Hee Seung Hwang, Sanghyuk Chun, Xindi Wu et al.
- **标签**: `cs.CL`
- **AI摘要**: 提出MERLIN方法改进线性注意力
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Self-attention plays a key role in long-sequence modeling, but its quadratic time complexity fundamentally limits the scaling of Large Language Models. Recently, Multi-State Linear Attention (MSLA) mechanisms have been proposed to make sequence modeling more efficient by splitting the context into segments and compressing each in its own memory state. MSLA aggregates the information extracted from each memory state using only the mean alignment between a query and each segment's keys, ignoring how the alignment is distributed within the segment. In order to model the relevance of a segment to a query more comprehensively and incorporate the variance of the alignment in the aggregation, we propose MERLIN (MomEnt Routing for multi-state LINear attention), a method that tracks the first and second moments of each segment's keys. Using the moments of keys, a query can recover both the mean and the variance of its alignment with each segment to perform a more content-aware aggregation of per-state readouts. Experiments on multi-state variants of Gated DeltaNet-1.3B and Mamba-2-780M show that MERLIN consistently outperforms existing aggregation methods on various long-context tasks. In particular, MERLIN outperforms baselines on RULER needle-in-a-haystack and long-context QA (+8.3% for Gated DeltaNet-1.3B and +7.0% for Mamba-2-780M) and diverse subtasks in LongBench (+13.5% and +12.0%). It even outperforms baselines that consume 4x more memory. MERLIN provides a simple and general routing mechanism for improving long-context capabilities of MSLA models.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 动机清晰，实验充分，对长文本建模有一定提升
