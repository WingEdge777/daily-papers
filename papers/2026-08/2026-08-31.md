<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Large language models are increasingly used to author Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC), where a single insecure default can be deployed directly into production. Prior evaluations report raw vulnerability counts for model-generated IaC, but without a human baseline they cannot determine whether models are actually worse than engineers. Two negative results also emerge: deployability does not correlate with vulnerability ($r = 0.158$, $p = 0.625$), and classical complete-case Friedman testing is infeasible for realistic benchmark designs, motivating the Skillings-Mack statistic. <summary>原始摘要</summary>
