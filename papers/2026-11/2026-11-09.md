### 1. **[BitDecoding-FP4: Native FP4 KV Cache Decoding via Algorithm-System Co-Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09825v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 85/100 | **日期**: 2026-11-06
- **作者**: Dayou Du, Woon Yee Ng, Guangming Sheng et al.
- **标签**: `cs.AI` `cs.AR` `cs.CL`
- **AI摘要**: 提出FP4 KV缓存解码的软硬件协同设计
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

The key-value (KV) cache dominates memory capacity and bandwidth in long-context LLM serving. While 4-bit microscaling formats (NVFP4, MXFP4) shrink the cache and run attention on FP4 Tensor Cores, a fundamental gap remains between algorithmic quantization and system-aware acceleration: existing FP4 KV systems use vanilla quantization that causes severe accuracy drops, whereas accuracy-oriented 4-bit algorithms rely on vector codebooks whose lookup overhead degrades decoding throughput. We present BitDecoding-FP4, an algorithm--system co-design for accurate and efficient native FP4 KV decoding. We mitigate three error sources: Dual-Residual Query Quantization splits queries into two FP4 tensors, cutting query logit error from 60% to 1%; Underflow-Aware Probability Scaling prevents E4M3 scales from rounding long-context probabilities to zero; and Outlier-Aware Key Quantization combines static smoothing, Hadamard rotation, and scale search to suppress key outliers under token-wise scaling. At the system level, we remove the softmax bottleneck via transposed softmax with fused probability quantization and stacked query MMA on paged KV caches. Across dense and MoE models with GQA and MLA, BitDecoding-FP4 stays within 0.5 points of BF16 KV accuracy with NVFP4 (1 point with MXFP4), and tracks BF16 KV in RL training rollouts. In end-to-end serving on NVIDIA B300 with Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-30B-A3B, it delivers 2.4-3.0$\times$ higher peak serving throughput than BF16 KV. The implementation is available at https://github.com/OpenBitSys/BitDecoding-FP4.
</details>

### 2. **[RuACT: Recovering Inactive Rubrics as Adaptive Critics and Teachers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09597v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 83/100 | **日期**: 2026-11-06
- **作者**: Yijun Li, Zhengxi Lu, Zhipiao Liu et al.
- **标签**: `cs.CL`
- **AI摘要**: 提出RuACT方法激活强化学习中失效的评分标准。
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Rubric-based reinforcement learning (RL) extends RL with verifiable rewards to open-ended tasks by scoring each response against query-specific rubrics. Under group-relative optimization, however, a rubric that every sampled response fails (unreachable) or passes (saturated) gives all responses the same outcome and no learning signal; in Qwen3-4B medical training, 60.88% of query-rubric observations are inactive. We find that the two states lack different supervision: stating an unreachable rubric in the prompt raises its satisfaction from 1.13% to 91.25%, whereas responses that all pass a saturated rubric still differ in quality. We therefore propose RuACT (Rubrics as Adaptive Critics and Teachers), which assigns each inactive rubric the role that supplies what it lacks. Rubric as Teacher uses unreachable rubrics as privileged information for reward-gated on-policy self-distillation, and Rubric as Critic decomposes saturated rubrics into child rubrics that separate current responses while sharing the parent's weight. RuACT outperforms GRPO, rubric reweighting, and rubric-guided exploration on all four medical and scientific benchmarks with Qwen3-4B and achieves the best average with Qwen3-1.7B. On initially unreachable rubrics, unguided recovery rises from 35.02% to 42.86%, while child refinement improves reward-quality alignment on independent rollouts from 57.74% to 60.77%. Code is available at https://github.com/ZJU-REAL/RuACT.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 方法创新有效，实验充分，对大模型强化学习有实用价值。

### 3. **[A Tight Hierarchy for Chain of Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09759v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-11-06
- **作者**: Thanh Le, A. Pavan, N. V. Vinodchandran
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.CC`
- **AI摘要**: 建立了链式思考推理的紧密计算复杂度层次定理。
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning has emerged as a powerful paradigm for extending the computational capabilities of transformer-based models. A growing body of recent work has begun to uncover the fine-grained computational power of transformers augmented with CoT, as well as their connections to classical complexity theory. In this work, we advance this line of research by establishing the first hierarchy theorem for CoT-based computation. Our main result shows that for any reasonable resource bound $r(n) \geq n$ and any $\varepsilon &gt; 0$, \[ \mathrm{CoT}(r(n)) \subsetneq \mathrm{CoT}(r(n)^{1+\varepsilon}), \] where $\mathrm{CoT}(r(n))$ denotes the class of decision problems solvable by transformers using $O(r(n))$ CoT reasoning steps on inputs of length $n$. Our result shows that increasing the number of CoT reasoning steps provably yields strictly greater computational power, establishing CoT steps as a fundamental resource in transformer-based computation. Unlike classical hierarchy theorems, which are typically proved via diagonalization, our approach leverages known coarse-grained simulations between CoT-augmented transformers and Turing machines in both directions. Building on these connections, we adapt the classical padding technique from complexity theory to this setting to obtain a tight hierarchy. A key technical contribution of our work is the implementation of padding within the transformer-based CoT model.

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Language-driven computer-aided design (CAD) generation enables the creation of editable parametric models from text, yet geometric correctness alone does not ensure physical feasibility. We present PhyCAD, a framework for physics-aware Text-to-CAD generation that trains language models to produce executable CadQuery programs under geometric and physical constraints. To support this setting, we construct a Text-to-CAD dataset that links parametric CAD models with geometric specifications, loading conditions, and physical constraints. We further develop an automated CAD--CAE verifier that combines program execution, geometric checks, and finite element analysis to provide feedback for model training. A key learning challenge is to prioritize joint satisfaction of geometric and physical constraints while retaining informative feedback on partial progress. A flat aggregation of heterogeneous rewards can obscure this priority, allowing improvements in individual criteria to compensate for unmet design requirements. We therefore introduce a Hierarchical Balanced Advantage for Group Relative Policy Optimization, grouping candidates into levels according to their verification status and combining between-level progress with objective-wise peer ranking within each level. This formulation preserves cross-level priority in the training signal while providing fine-grained feedback for satisfying constraints and improving feasible designs. Experiments across multiple language models show that PhyCAD consistently improves joint geometric and physical constraint satisfaction. These results demonstrate the value of verifiable engineering feedback for advancing language-driven CAD generation beyond geometric reconstruction toward physics-aware design.

- **AI摘要**: 提出强耗散随机内插器以实现稳定气候模拟

<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Predicting the long-term behavior of partially observed dynamical systems such as the coupled atmosphere-ocean system is central to societal decision-making. Motivated by the success of deep learning emulators in weather forecasting, data-driven climate models represent a promising approach. However, climate emulation poses distinct challenges: models must account for unresolved fast variables introduced by unavoidable coarse-graining, reproduce long-term statistics, and remain stable over arbitrarily long rollouts and under external perturbations. These requirements motivate stochastic data-driven emulators, but modern generative models lack stability guarantees. We introduce forced-dissipative stochastic interpolants, a generative framework for learning stochastic reduced dynamics. Its architecture uses structural constraints inspired by turbulent dynamics, which provide stability by design while retaining the flexibility of stochastic interpolants to represent complex probabilistic dynamics. Building on the theory of Markov-chain stability, we establish a Foster--Lyapunov drift condition and prove geometric ergodicity for our emulator, together with stability under steady external forcing. We evaluate the framework on the two-timescale Lorenz-96 system, a prototype of partially observed multiscale turbulence. The resulting emulator remains stable over long integrations and under finite-amplitude forcing, with accurate prediction of forced responses of both the mean and variance.
